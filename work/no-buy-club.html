No-Buy Club: from “I want it now” to “do I still want it?”

I designed and built a small app that helps people stop buying on impulse. Every urge goes on ice for a cooling-off period before it becomes a decision, slips are logged without guilt, and the money you don’t spend grows towards a goal you care about.

Role
Concept, UI design and build
For
Anyone who wants to stop buying on impulse
Tools
HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Output
One self-contained HTML file, no account, data stays in the browser
the-pain-point.txt_▢x

The pain point

Buying feels good right now, and saving feels like nothing

Impulse buying is about the small hit of excitement more than the thing itself. A new season, a sale, a bad day, an ad at midnight: the urge is quick, the checkout is one tap away, and the feeling fades soon after the parcel arrives.

I wanted a tool that works with that, not against it. It should not lecture, and it should not need willpower alone. Three problems stood in the way:

impulse.txt_▢x

The urge is faster than the second thought

Online shops are built to make buying instant. There is no natural pause between wanting something and owning it.

guilt.txt_▢x

One slip feels like failing

When a single purchase wipes out a streak, it is tempting to give up on the whole thing. Shame is a poor motivator.

motivation.txt_▢x

Saved money is invisible

Money you don’t spend leaves no trace. Without something to watch grow, not buying feels like missing out.

How might we make not buying something feel as rewarding as buying it?

no-buy-club.exe_▢x

The idea: put it on ice

Log what you want, with its price and why you want it right now. It sits on ice for 15 days. Then the app asks one question: do you still want it?

Try it

  1. Answer the verdict. One sample item is ready to decide. Let it go, or ask for time to buy it.
  2. Put something on ice. Add an item and watch the money stay yours.
  3. Read the numbers. Scroll to the stats and achievements.
  4. Make it yours. Edit the goal, the days on ice and the currency.

Everything in the demo is made-up sample data. What you change stays in your browser only.

Kind rules, honest numbers

ice.txt_▢x

Ice first, decide later

Every urge is parked for 15 days, a number you can change. If the wish has faded by then, the price joins your goal.

green-light.txt_▢x

A week to buy it, no guilt

If you still want it, you get seven days to actually buy it. Passing on it after that counts double, because you had permission.

honesty.txt_▢x

Slips are data

“I bought something” logs it and restarts the streak. Your longest streak is kept and your goal total never goes backwards. A monthly “delusion index” counts only the impulse buys.

What was delivered

A small, kawaii-styled app with a shiba mascot, a goal bar, stats and achievements. It is one HTML file with no account and no server, so it is private by default.

one-file.html_▢x

One self-contained file

HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single page. Open it anywhere, on a phone or a computer.

private.sys_▢x

Your data stays with you

Everything is stored in the browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is nothing to sign up for.

backup.json_▢x

Save and restore

A backup file or a copy to the clipboard, plus a gentle reminder. Euro, Brazilian real and US dollar are built in.

Problem solved

guilt.fixed_▢x

motivation.fixed_▢x
