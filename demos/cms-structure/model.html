CMS structure · Referral portal

One content model, many brands

Every referral portal is assembled from the same nested entries, from the Promotion at the top down to each reward row. A brand only swaps its Theme entry and its copy; the structure stays exactly the same.

The content model

How a portal nests, entry by entry

Read it top down. Each line is a reference from a parent entry to the entries it links. Every card lists its fields with their type.

How to read this

One Promotion entry is the root of a portal. It links the brand's Theme, the Portal itself and, when needed, follow-up pages.

Numbers show the order of sections on the page. The count badge shows how many linked entries the Nimbelo demo uses.
