Where the road goes next

Pages that search engines can finally read.

The project turned 85 inconsistent pages into one consistent, fully described set. Here is what that sets up.

Searchable from the first crawlEvery page now has a unique title and description inside the length where search results show them in full, a correct canonical URL and clear crawler directives. Pages stop competing with their own duplicates and start describing themselves properly.
Better first impressionsA complete social preview and a designed share image on every page means links look intentional when they are pasted into chats and feeds, which is where a lot of referral traffic starts.
Results you can measureThe audit leaves a clean baseline of 17 scored fields per page. Impressions, click-through rate and rankings can now be tracked against it in search analytics, and any new page can be scored the same way on day one.
