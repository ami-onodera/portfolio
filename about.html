But you can call me Ami. I’m a designer who codes: I make brands look good on the web, and then I make sure nothing gets lost between the design and the live page. And I’ve been doing it for 15+ years.

In my current position, I design, build and launch B2B and B2C web experiences for partner brands: landing pages, customer portals and branded widgets. HTML/CSS, Contentful, Figma, JSON configuration and Material UI theming are my daily tools, with Claude as my sidekick for prototyping and automating the repetitive bits. I also own design QA and release readiness, which is how 163 hosted pages stayed consistent from first draft to live link.

I started as an art director, then created content for nine brands for P&G Brasil, working through an agency. Later, as a project manager at Language International, I led a team of software developers and built a website translated into 27 languages. That path is why I bridge design and delivery: I care how a brand looks, and how it actually gets built.
