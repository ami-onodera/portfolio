About

Hi, I’m Camila.

I’m a Senior Web & Brand Designer in Berlin, with 15+ years across digital design, brand communication, art direction and web production.

At Aklamio, which builds referral-marketing programmes for brands in telecom, energy, banking, insurance and more, I design and ship B2B web experiences: referral landing pages, customer portals and branded widgets. I work hands-on in Figma, HTML/CSS, Contentful, JSON configuration and Material UI theming, use generative AI and vibe coding with Claude to prototype and automate, and I look after design QA and release readiness. So far that adds up to 40+ hosted pages from 67 integration tickets.

I started as an art director at Editora Abril, then ran community and content for nine brands at Procter & Gamble Brasil. As a project manager at Language International I led a team of software developers. That path is why I bridge design and delivery: I care how a brand looks, and how it gets built and shipped.
